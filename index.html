How to play

Steering. Push the stick with your left thumb in the direction you want to drive. The car turns until it is heading that way. It can start anywhere on the left of the screen — the stick comes to your thumb.

Speed. Your car accelerates on its own. The BRAKE button is your only speed control, and you will need it on the tighter circuits.

FIRE uses whatever item you're carrying. The button shows what you've got.

🚀 Missile flies forward and spins out whoever it hits.
🛢️ Oil drops behind you for whoever is chasing.
⚡ Turbo is an instant burst of speed.

Yellow squares are upgrades — engine, tyres and roll cage. You keep them for the whole championship, so grabbing them early pays off for the rest of the season.

Glowing arrows on the tarmac are speed strips. Free speed if you can hit them.

Stay on the tarmac. The dirt is slow.

Finish in the top three to move on to the next circuit.

Controls

Push the stick on the left in the direction you want to drive — the car turns to follow it. The buttons on the right are brake and fire. Your car speeds up on its own.

How sharply the car swings round to the direction you're pushing. Turn it up if the steering feels vague.

Turn this off if explosions and bumps make you queasy.
