How to play

Steering. Left and right on the cross turn your car, for as long as you hold them. Let go and it holds the line it's on. It's a steering wheel, not a compass — you're turning the car, not pointing it.

BRAKE is the bottom of the cross. Your car speeds up on its own, so braking is your only way to slow down — and it also lets you turn about a quarter quicker, which is how you get round the tight stuff.

TURBO is a burst of speed from a meter that refills when you aren't using it. About two seconds of boost when full. Everyone on the grid has one.

🛢 OIL lays a slick behind you, for whoever is chasing. You pick slicks up from crates and can carry three.

Crates hold oil slicks and turbo refills.

Yellow squares are upgrades — engine, tyres and roll cage. You keep them for the whole championship, even on races you don't win, so they're always worth the detour off the racing line.

Glowing arrows on the tarmac are speed strips. Free speed if you can hit them.

Stay on the tarmac. The dirt is slow.

Finish in the top three to move on to the next circuit.

Controls
